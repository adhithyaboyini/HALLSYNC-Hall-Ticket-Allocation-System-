HOW TO ENABLE MULTI-SELECT SUBJECTS MACRO
==========================================

1. Open Student_Info_Template.xlsm in Microsoft Excel
2. Press Alt + F11  (opens VBA Editor)
3. In the left panel, double-click 'ThisWorkbook'
4. Copy and paste the full content of ThisWorkbook.bas into that window
5. Right-click the project > Insert > Module
6. Copy and paste the full content of Module1.bas into that window
7. Press Ctrl+S to save (keep as .xlsm)
8. Close the VBA Editor
9. If prompted, click 'Enable Macros' when opening the file

RESULT:
Clicking any cell in the Subjects column (J7:J106)
will open a popup with checkboxes for:
  Maths, Science, Social Science, Biology,
  Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science
Ticking subjects fills them comma-separated into the cell automatically.
